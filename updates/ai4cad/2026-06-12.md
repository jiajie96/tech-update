# AI for CAD Weekly Update — June 12, 2026

## Highlights

- **PTC NEXT Chicago: 12 new AI agents and two cross-portfolio platforms**: PTC unveiled PTC Orbit and PTC Jetstream alongside a new AI platform, 12 AI agents, 10 integrations, and AI-assisted engineering updates across Creo, Onshape, and Windchill — the week's biggest AI-in-CAD story.
- **Agentic AI lands across Autodesk 2027 AEC tools**: Autodesk Assistant is now embedded in Revit and Civil 3D 2027, bringing geometry-aware, model-aware agents that understand BIM and CAD logic.
- **Generative rendering goes mainstream**: SketchUp became the first major CAD program to ship generative rendering (SketchUp AI Render via Trimble), one of three AI features expected in every CAD program this year.

## Detailed Updates

### PTC NEXT: AI across the product lifecycle
At its PTC NEXT event in Chicago this June, PTC announced PTC Orbit, a cloud-native, AI-maintained "as-maintained" system of intelligence unifying asset data across PLM, ERP, CRM, IoT, EAM, and FSM; and PTC Jetstream for traceable design review feedback integrated with PLM, ALM, and CAD. Creo gained AI-assisted engineering with simulation-driven optimization and electrification support; Onshape expanded AI design feedback and robotics simulation; Windchill added AI-assisted PLM workflows. (Sources: DEVELOP3D, PR Newswire)

### Autodesk Assistant becomes agentic in the 2027 lineup
Autodesk embedded its Assistant across the 2027 AEC portfolio, including Revit 2027 and Civil 3D 2027. The agentic assistant is geometry- and model-aware, able to reason over BIM and CAD logic rather than just answering documentation questions — a meaningful step beyond chat-style copilots. (Source: ARKANCE)

### Graebert ARES 2027: AI push plus Forma integration
Graebert's ARES 2027 ecosystem of DWG-native CAD products delivers a broad set of new AI features along with Autodesk Forma cloud integration, bringing AI workflows to the large installed base of DWG users outside Autodesk's own products. (Source: Architosh)

### SOLIDWORKS companions LEO and MARIE on the way
Dassault Systèmes detailed two Virtual Companions coming to SOLIDWORKS Design in 2026 via the 3DEXPERIENCE platform: command prediction, automated generative engineering drawings, parametric models from a picture, and 3D CAD creation from text and voice prompts. (Source: SOLIDWORKS)

### Text-to-CAD matures past the mesh era
The text-to-CAD segment continues to professionalize: tools like AdamCAD and Zoo now output STEP files rather than just STL meshes, with some generating basic parametric features. DraftAid automates 2D drawings and Leo AI builds full assemblies from sketches or specs. Reviews this week emphasize the gap between marketing and production readiness is narrowing but still real. (Sources: The CAD Hub, Leo AI)

### AI rendering and MEP automation in AEC
Chaos's Veras continues to lead diffusion-based visualization plugged directly into CAD/BIM platforms, while MEP-focused Aurivus reports up to 70% modeling-time reduction — a pharmaceutical plant with thousands of pipes processed in two weeks versus months of manual modeling. (Source: Chaos blog)
