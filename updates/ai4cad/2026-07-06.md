# AI for CAD Weekly Update — July 6, 2026

A relatively quiet week for AI-in-CAD product news; the most substantive items are ecosystem analyses and platform updates rather than major launches.

## Highlights

- **SOLIDWORKS previews LEO and MARIE virtual companions**: Dassault Systèmes is rolling out two AI assistants for SOLIDWORKS Design on the 3DEXPERIENCE platform — LEO for mechanical design workflows, MARIE for materials science and chemistry expertise.
- **Design review beats generative design on ROI**: New industry analysis argues AI-powered design review is the mature, deployable application of AI in engineering today, while generative CAD is still catching up to its hype.
- **ALLPLAN ships 2026 releases with new AI and BIM tools**: The BIM vendor's latest product line targets productivity, accuracy, and sustainability across AEC project workflows.

## Detailed Updates

### LEO and MARIE come to SOLIDWORKS
Two new virtual companions are coming to SOLIDWORKS Design in 2026 via the 3DEXPERIENCE platform. LEO specializes in mechanical design and engineering workflow assistance, while MARIE brings expertise in materials science, hypothesis development, and chemistry — signaling Dassault's bet on domain-specialized AI copilots rather than a single general assistant. (solidworks.com)

### The state of text-to-CAD
Current text-to-CAD tools can take a description like "a steel bracket 150mm x 80mm with four M8 bolt holes" and generate a fully editable 3D model in seconds. Mid-2026 tool roundups place Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, and Zoo among the leaders for prompt-to-3D, with Leo AI and PROME.AI targeting engineering-grade workflows alongside incumbents like Fusion 360, Siemens NX, and SolidWorks. (simutecra.com, buildmvpfast.com)

### Design review is where the ROI is
CoLab's analysis of AI CAD in 2026 argues that generative design is not where the clearest value lies today — AI-assisted design review is the more mature, immediately deployable application. PTC's 2026 CAD trends report supports the broader point: manufacturers adopting AI-assisted design are seeing measurable advantages in quality, cost, and time-to-market. NASA-backed studies still show generative-design components can be 30–50% lighter than traditionally designed parts at required strength. (colabsoftware.com, fabrixon.com)

### ALLPLAN 2026 releases land
ALLPLAN announced its 2026 product releases with new AI and BIM features aimed at productivity, accuracy, and sustainability across design-to-build workflows — part of a broader "BIM 6.0" trend merging AI, digital twins, IoT, and automated project delivery. (engineering.com)

### AEC AI adoption: small base, big intent
New research from Chaos on AI in architecture finds only 27% of AEC firms currently use AI in operations, but 94% of adopters plan to increase investment — suggesting rapid near-term expansion of AI design tooling. AI clash detection in BIM systems is a leading use case, surfacing conflicts before they become expensive construction problems. (blog.chaos.com, designheed.com)

### Autodesk's Wonder 3D continues rollout
Autodesk's Wonder 3D (launched in Flow Studio in March) remains the reference point for text/image-to-3D in the Autodesk ecosystem, generating editable 3D assets across all Flow Studio tiers, with recent Maya and 3ds Max updates adding further AI workflow enhancements. No major new Autodesk AI announcements this week. (adsknews.autodesk.com)
