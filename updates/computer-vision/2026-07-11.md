# Computer Vision Weekly Update — July 11, 2026

## Highlights

- **Meta launches Muse Image**: Meta Superintelligence Labs' first image-generation model rolled out in Meta AI this week. It plans layouts before rendering, pulls real-time web context via Muse Spark, blends multiple visual references, and renders clean in-image text — enabling legible infographics and how-to guides.
- **GPT-5.6 goes GA with ChatGPT Images 2.0 leading text rendering**: OpenAI's July 9 GA release strengthens multimodal capability, with ChatGPT Images 2.0 currently regarded as best-in-class for precise multilingual text and infographic layouts.
- **Grok Imagine Video 1.5 joins a consolidating video-gen field**: With Sora 2 officially discontinued in April, Google's Veo 3.1 leads video generation, now challenged by xAI's Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and chatter around Alibaba's Happy Horse 1.0.

## Detailed Updates

### Meta Muse Image: reasoning before rendering
Meta rolled out Muse Image, its first image model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, now available in Meta AI. The model "thinks through" prompts — planning layout, looking up real-time web context via its pairing with Muse Spark, and blending multiple visual references in one generation. Its clean in-image text rendering targets the infographic/design use case where diffusion models have historically struggled. (Meta Newsroom)

### The video-generation field consolidates
Following the official discontinuation of Sora 2 on April 26, 2026, Google's Veo 3.1 stands as the premier video-generation model, with xAI's newly released Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and Alibaba's rumored Happy Horse 1.0 signaling continued competition in multimodal video workflows. Mid-year retrospectives point to image/video models defining the first half of 2026 as much as text models did in 2025. (Hedra, x.ai, WaveSpeed)

### NVIDIA's Alpamayo opens up reasoning-based driving vision
NVIDIA's Alpamayo portfolio — including Alpamayo R1, the first open reasoning vision-language-action model for autonomous driving, and the AlpaSim open simulation blueprint for high-fidelity AV testing — gives researchers an open stack for Level-4-capable autonomy. Meanwhile Waymo's London operations advanced to on-street autonomous driving under safety operators. (NVIDIA)

### Medical imaging: autonomous scanning and novel sensors
NVIDIA and GE HealthCare are building physics-based digital twins of medical environments to train autonomous diagnostic-imaging systems. Separately, Midjourney announced an unexpected healthcare venture: an underwater ultrasound scanner using a ring of echolocating ultrasonic sensors to build a 3D map of the human body in about 60 seconds. (NVIDIA Newsroom, ZoneTechify)

### 3D vision research: VLMs meet 3D understanding
The research pipeline continues converging vision-language models with 3D perception: recent work spans 3D-grounded vision-language frameworks for robotic task planning, mamba-enhanced multi-view stereo with geometry-aware feature fusion, unified voxel-fusion frameworks for multimodal 3D detection, and encoder-free 3D multimodal architectures. The 3DV 2026 proceedings feature neural surface reconstruction and sparse-view CT recovery among active threads. (arXiv, IEEE 3DV)
