# Computer Vision Weekly Update — June 12, 2026

## Highlights

- **CVPR 2026 Best Paper: D4RT from Google DeepMind/UCL/Oxford**: A single transformer that reconstructs dynamic 4D scenes from ordinary video — jointly producing depth, 3D tracks, and camera poses up to 100x faster than prior pose estimation, awarded June 9 in Denver.
- **CVPR by the numbers**: 4,089 papers accepted from a record 16,092 submissions; image/video synthesis, vision-language reasoning, and embodied AI dominated, with generation-related work growing from ~14% to ~22% of highlighted papers.
- **Waymo opens four new cities at once**: Fully autonomous ride-hailing launched in Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, and Orlando — 10 commercial metros total, targeting one million rides per week by year-end.

## Detailed Updates

### D4RT wins CVPR 2026 Best Paper
"Efficiently Reconstructing Dynamic Scenes One D4RT at a Time," led by Chuhan Zhang during a Google DeepMind internship with UCL and Oxford, took top honors. One encoder-decoder transformer replaces separate networks for depth, tracking, and pose, reaching ~200 fps for pose and processing a minute of video in seconds on one TPU — directly relevant to AR, robotics, and autonomous driving. (Sources: Newswise, Digg)

### CVPR 2026 trends: embodied AI is the dark horse
From 16,092 submissions, the committee saw the most papers in image/video synthesis, vision-language reasoning, multimodal learning, 3D from multiview/sensors, and medical vision. Highlighted-paper analysis shows robotics, humanoid loco-manipulation, VLA models, and autonomous driving emerging as a unified embodied-AI focus. Other notable awards: ChordEdit, a training-free one-step high-fidelity image editor enabling real-time edits, and NitroGen, an open foundation model for generalist gaming agents trained on 40,000 hours of gameplay across 1,000+ games. (Sources: CVPR, Bohrium)

### Apple's CVPR research slate
Apple presented STARFlow-V (end-to-end video generation with normalizing flows), TrajTok (trajectory tokens for video understanding), UniGen-1.5 (unified reward for image generation and editing), and Velox (4D geometry and appearance representations) at CVPR in Denver, June 3–7. (Sources: Apple ML Research, 9to5Mac)

### Veo 3.1 Lite Preview
Google launched Veo 3.1 Lite Preview, a cost-efficient video generation model for rapid iteration and high-volume applications — continuing the trend of "lite" generation tiers for production workloads. (Source: Google AI changelog)

### Waymo's multi-city expansion vs Tesla's scrutiny
Waymo launched public service in four cities simultaneously, scaling to hundreds of thousands of paid trips weekly across 10 metros. Tesla expanded Robotaxi in Austin, but a May Reuters investigation found Tesla inflated FSD safety figures by roughly 3x, and at least 17 Robotaxi accidents were recorded across 2025–26 — keeping the lidar-versus-vision-only architecture debate unresolved. (Sources: autoconnectedcar.com, VaaSBlock)
