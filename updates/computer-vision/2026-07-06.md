# Computer Vision Weekly Update — July 6, 2026

## Highlights

- **Half-year report: video models grow up**: The first half of 2026 saw image models cross into production-grade territory while video models gained native audio and real camera control, led by ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 and Google's Veo 3.1.
- **Sora sunset continues**: OpenAI discontinued the Sora web and app experiences on April 26, and the Sora API will shut down September 24, 2026 — leaving the text-to-video field to ByteDance, Google, Alibaba, and Kuaishou.
- **CVPR 2026 shatters records**: The year's flagship vision conference closed with record attendance and submissions, spotlighting 3D/4D scene reconstruction and driving foundation models.

## Detailed Updates

### Video generation leaderboard, mid-2026
As of late June, the Artificial Analysis Video Arena text-to-video-with-audio board is led by ByteDance's Dreamina Seedance 2.0 (Elo ~1,219), followed by Alibaba's HappyHorse models and Kuaishou's Kling 3.0. Seedance 2.0, Veo 3.1, and Kling 3.0 all produce video with synchronized audio in a single pass; Veo is the only one reliably generating 48kHz dialogue. Seedance 2.0 remains the first model to achieve unified audio-video joint generation in one pass, though ByteDance is still delaying its developer API amid unresolved Hollywood copyright disputes. (poyo.ai, hedra.com)

### OpenAI winds down Sora
OpenAI confirmed the Sora web and app experiences were discontinued April 26, 2026, with the Sora API to follow on September 24. Sora 2 remains technically significant but now carries a clear sunset timeline, effectively ceding the consumer video-generation race. (poyo.ai)

### Image generation: a segmented market
OpenAI's GPT Image 2 tops the Artificial Analysis Image Arena (Elo 1339), while Google's Nano Banana Pro (4096×4096 native), Black Forest Labs' FLUX.2 (open-source Dev variant), Midjourney V8.1 (default model since June 10, 2048×2048 default renders), and Stable Diffusion 3.5 each hold distinct niches. The mid-2026 landscape is segmented by specialized strengths rather than dominated by a single tool. (tech-insider.org, storytool.io)

### CVPR 2026 and the 3D vision wave
CVPR 2026 closed with record-breaking attendance and submissions. Driving-related vision research is consolidating around foundation models: the DriveX workshop series is designing models natively aware of cooperative perception and V2X constraints, with 3D/4D Gaussian splatting for scene reconstruction as a core topic. Recent arXiv work includes DynamicVGGT (dynamic point maps for 4D driving-scene reconstruction), DriveTok (3D scene tokenization for unified multi-view reconstruction), and HorizonStream (long-horizon attention for streaming 3D reconstruction). (cvpr.thecvf.com, arxiv.org)

### NPC and character vision-speech stacks go on-device
NVIDIA ACE now runs fully on-device with no cloud roundtrip and shipped its first production-grade text-to-speech model, while Resemble.ai's 350M-parameter Chatterbox v1.0.0 enables zero-shot voice cloning from short samples — both feeding real-time character perception/generation pipelines in games and simulation. (blog.imseankim.com)
