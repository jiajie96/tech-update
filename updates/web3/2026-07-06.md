# Web3 Weekly Update — July 6, 2026

## Highlights

- **Bitcoin rebounds above $62K on soft jobs data**: After the worst June for BTC in four years, weak US employment numbers (57K jobs vs. 113K forecast) stoked rate-cut hopes and pushed Bitcoin from ~$61.5K to a brief $62,117 on July 3.
- **H2 upgrade season: Glamsterdam, Alpenglow, Beryl**: Ethereum's biggest upgrade since The Merge, Solana's 100–150ms finality overhaul, and Base's completed Beryl hard fork make protocol upgrades — not prices — the key variable for the second half of 2026.
- **MiCA transition closes with 244 authorized firms**: The EU's crypto regulatory transition period officially ended, while the UK FCA finalized its framework with mandatory licensing from 2027.

## Detailed Updates

### Markets: extreme fear meets a green July
Bitcoin opened at $61,492.99 on July 3, up 2.5% from the previous day, and briefly touched $62,117 before hitting resistance. The rally is attributed to the BLS reporting just 57,000 new June jobs — roughly half of forecasts — which investors read as a signal for monetary easing. The Fear and Greed Index rose to 21 but remains in extreme-fear territory after Bitcoin's worst June in four years. (Yahoo Finance, CoinDesk)

### Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade
Glamsterdam, slated for H2 2026, is Ethereum's most significant protocol upgrade since The Merge. It introduces enshrined proposer-builder separation (ePBS) to reduce centralization risk, expands data throughput, and cuts database bloat — positioning Ethereum for stablecoin settlement and real-world-asset use cases. (digitaltoday.co.kr, tradingview.com)

### Solana's Alpenglow targets sub-second finality
Alpenglow, arriving in H2 2026 alongside the Agave 4.1 validator client, aims to cut transaction finality from ~12.8 seconds to 100–150 milliseconds under optimal conditions — one of three major protocol-level updates planned for Solana. Base has already implemented its Beryl hard fork, and Avalanche is restructuring around institutional demand and tokenized assets. (thecoinrepublic.com, news.cryptos.com)

### Regulation: MiCA done, CLARITY contested, FCA finalized
The EU's MiCA transition period closed with 244 crypto firms authorized across Europe. In the US, Senators Lummis and Warren are clashing over the CLARITY Act — Warren arguing it opens the door to crypto crime. The UK FCA finalized its crypto framework covering trading platforms and stablecoin issuers, with mandatory licensing beginning 2027. Separately, the FHFA has ordered Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac to prepare to count cryptocurrency as an asset for mortgages. (DeFi Planet, investingnews.com)

### Web3 founders pivot to Wall Street
A new report finds RWA tokenization has overtaken DeFi as Web3 startups' primary focus, with founders increasingly building for institutional finance — tokenization, regulated infrastructure, and enterprise adoption — rather than crypto-native markets. (CCN)

### Trump crypto entanglement deepens
Reporting this week highlights that Trump-linked crypto ventures pulled in over $1.4 billion in reported income in 2025, an unprecedented level of presidential financial entanglement with an industry the administration helps regulate. (DeFi Planet)
