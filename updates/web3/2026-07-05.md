# Web3 Weekly Update — July 5, 2026

## Highlights

- **Open USD launches with 140+ partners, rattling stablecoin incumbents**: A consortium including BlackRock, Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Coinbase, and Shopify unveiled Open USD (OUSD), a collectively governed stablecoin set to launch natively on Solana. Circle's stock fell 13% within hours as markets priced in a credible challenger to USDC and USDT — a sign that stablecoins are becoming shared financial infrastructure rather than single-issuer products.
- **SEC and CFTC deliver long-awaited crypto asset taxonomy**: The SEC published an interpretation clarifying how federal securities laws apply to crypto assets, sorting tokens into digital commodities, collectibles, tools, stablecoins, and securities, and addressing airdrops, staking, and wrapping. After a decade of regulation-by-enforcement, this is the clearest classification framework US markets have had.
- **Ethereum's Glamsterdam enters its final phase**: Developers locked in EIP-7732 and EIP-7928 for mainnet activation in H2 2026, confirming the largest protocol overhaul since The Merge, with a 200M gas limit target and enshrined proposer-builder separation.

## Detailed Updates

### Open USD consortium takes aim at Tether and Circle
On June 30, Open Standard announced Open USD, a stablecoin backed by more than 140 launch partners spanning banks (BNY, BBVA, DBS, Standard Chartered), payments giants (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), and crypto-native firms (Coinbase, Aave, Morpho, MetaMask). Businesses can mint and redeem OUSD with no fees or volume caps, with most reserve earnings flowing back to partners. Governance sits with a board of partner organizations rather than a single issuer, and the token launches natively on Solana later this year. Fortune, CoinDesk, and The Defiant covered the launch; CoinDesk noted Circle shares slid sharply on the news.

### SEC clarifies application of securities laws to crypto
The SEC's new interpretation, issued jointly in spirit with the CFTC under Chairmen Paul Atkins and Michael Selig, lays out a coherent token taxonomy and explains how non-security crypto assets can enter and exit investment-contract status. It also directly addresses airdrops, protocol mining, protocol staking, and wrapped assets. Coming after 2025's legislative groundwork, this guidance is the operational rulebook the industry has been waiting for, per the SEC's press release and Yahoo Finance's weekly regulation roundup.

### Glamsterdam upgrade reaches final devnet stage
Ethereum developers confirmed this week that Glamsterdam has entered its final devnet stage ahead of a second-half 2026 mainnet activation. Headline changes include enshrined proposer-builder separation (removing the third-party relays that currently touch over 88% of blocks), a roughly 3x gas limit increase toward a 200M floor, and cheaper base transactions — a standard ETH transfer could get up to 71% cheaper, per ethereum.org and OpenPR reporting.

### Solana ships on-chain governance as Alpenglow nears
Solana launched on-chain governance this week, letting stakers open proposals and vote on network changes for the first time. Meanwhile the Alpenglow upgrade, which would cut finality from ~12 seconds to roughly 150 milliseconds, remains on track for a Q3 rollout, according to The Defiant and Yahoo Finance.

### BNB Chain and AWS launch BNB Agent Studio
BNB Chain partnered with AWS to launch BNB Agent Studio, letting developers deploy autonomous on-chain AI agents — each with its own wallet — from a single prompt in about 15 minutes, per Hipther's July 2 roundup. It's a notable convergence of the AI-agent and crypto-wallet stacks.

### Solana RWAs hit record high; June exploit losses ease
Solana's tokenized real-world asset ecosystem reached an all-time high of $3.41 billion, per DeFi Planet's weekend roundup. The same report noted exploit losses declined from $81.7M in May to $75.9M in June, and Bitcoin reclaimed $63,000 to reverse end-June losses.

### Funding: derivatives DEX EXT raises $12.5M
Per crypto fundraising trackers, DeFi derivatives platform EXT raised $12.5M from investors including eToro and Jump Crypto, a Solana AI analytics project raised $8M (Maven 11, Spartan Group), and Base-ecosystem DEX QUID raised $2.25M — consistent with a July market where investors favor evidence over narratives.
