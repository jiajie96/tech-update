# Web3 Weekly Update — July 11, 2026

## Highlights

- **SEC's "Regulation Crypto" proposal slated for July**: The SEC updated its agenda to release a crypto rulemaking proposal as soon as this month, establishing safe harbors for on-chain activities including DeFi and tokenized securities — startups under $5M valuation could raise up to $75M via qualifying crypto investment contracts.
- **Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade advances**: Devnet-5 testing is underway with public testnet targeted for July/August and mainnet realistically in Q3. Headline EIPs: 7732 (enshrined proposer-builder separation) and 7928 (block-level access lists, groundwork for parallel execution).
- **Robinhood Chain surges past Base**: Days after mainnet launch, Robinhood Chain's daily DEX volume topped $560M with ~200K daily active addresses, making it the second-largest Uniswap deployment chain.

## Detailed Updates

### SEC safe-harbor rulemaking approaches
The SEC's "Regulation Crypto" proposal could land as soon as July 2026, creating safe harbors and exemptions for certain on-chain activities including DeFi and tokenized securities. Eligible startups (valued under $5M, within their first four years) could raise up to $75M via qualifying crypto investment contracts. Separately, a new draft of the Clarity Act may emerge within days in a push for late-July action, though bipartisan support remains uncertain. (Cryptonomist, Latham & Watkins tracker)

### Glamsterdam takes shape
Ethereum's next major upgrade, Glamsterdam, is in Devnet-5 testing with public testnet deployment targeted for July or August. Developers describe Q3 as the realistic mainnet window (with possible slippage to Q4). The upgrade centers on EIP-7732, introducing enshrined proposer-builder separation (ePBS), and EIP-7928 block-level access lists — the groundwork for parallel transaction processing. (KuCoin market report)

### Robinhood Chain's explosive start
Days after its mainnet launch, Robinhood Chain passed $560M in daily DEX volume and neared 200K daily active addresses, overtaking Base to become the second-largest chain by Uniswap deployment volume — a striking demonstration of brokerage distribution advantage in on-chain markets.

### Banks pilot tokenized assets on Swift's shared ledger
Swift announced its blockchain-powered shared ledger is ready for initial deployment, with 17 banks — including HSBC, UBS, Wells Fargo, and Citi — preparing to pilot live transactions using tokenized digital assets. It's one of the most concrete institutional tokenization milestones to date. (DeFi Planet roundup)

### DeFi: Ethena cuts USDe mint/redeem fees to zero
Ethena Labs introduced zero-fee USDC minting and redemption for approved users of its USDe synthetic dollar protocol, sharpening competition among yield-bearing stable assets.

### Markets and enforcement
The crypto market gained 1.9% on July 10, with global capitalization reaching $2.25 trillion on $60.3B volume; Bitcoin pushed toward $65K. Polymarket is reportedly eyeing margin trading. On the enforcement side, Interpol busted a $122M crypto scheme, and Empery Digital's 8-K disclosed the sale of 1,400 BTC (~$87.1M at $62,200 average) to retire debt and cover litigation costs — a reminder that corporate treasury unwinds are now part of market structure. (TradingView/99Bitcoins, KuCoin)
