# Web3 Weekly Update — July 3, 2026

## Highlights

- **MiCA grandfathering ends**: July 1 marked the deadline for any exchange, custodian, or broker serving EU clients without CASP authorization to wind down services. National regulators can now impose fines up to €15 million or 12.5% of annual turnover — the EU's crypto regime is fully in force.
- **Ethereum's institutional front door opens**: A new non-profit launched July 1 — funded by Bitmine, Sharplink, and Joe Lubin — to guide major financial institutions into the Ethereum ecosystem, as the Glamsterdam upgrade (parallel execution, PBS) progresses through H1.
- **Solana's institutional week**: MoneyGram joined as a validator (June 22), Morgan Stanley amended Solana ETF filings revealing record-low 0.14% fees, and Moody's launched credit ratings for Solana tokenized assets — while co-founder Anatoly Yakovenko says the Alpenglow consensus upgrade could ship as early as Q3.

## Detailed Updates

### EU MiCA enforcement begins in earnest
The grandfathering era ended July 1: unauthorized crypto-asset service providers must exit the EU market, with penalties up to €15M or 12.5% of turnover. Coverage focused on which platforms hold CASP authorization for EU users. Meanwhile the UK's FCA consultation on cryptoasset perimeter guidance is addressing how DeFi interfaces and wallets fit the regulatory perimeter. ([CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/01/mica-deadline-hits-top-safe-crypto-platforms-for-eu-users-in-july-2026/), [Fintech & Digital Assets Blog](https://www.fintechanddigitalassets.com/2026/04/fca-consultation-on-cryptoasset-perimeter-guidance-key-points-for-defi-and-web3-user-interfaces-and-wallets/))

### Institutional rails multiply on both major chains
Ethereum's new "Institutional Front Door" non-profit aims to onboard major financial institutions; protocol-side, Glamsterdam (H1) targets throughput via parallel execution and proposer-builder separation, with Hegotá (H2) bringing Verkle Trees. On Solana: MoneyGram became a validator, Morgan Stanley's amended ETF filings showed 0.14% fees (cheapest crypto ETFs globally), and Moody's began rating Solana tokenized assets. ([CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/ethereum/latest-updates/), [Tatum](https://tatum.io/blog/blockchain-upgrades-2026), [Bitcoin Foundation](https://bitcoinfoundation.org/news/altcoins/top-solana-updates-in-2026-network-upgrades-ecosystem-growth-and-institutional-adoption-trends/))

### Banks take over stablecoin issuance
June's new stablecoin launches came from institutions with existing regulatory relationships rather than crypto-native startups — bank-issued and consortium coins from SoFi, MUFG, SMBC, Mizuho, and BANCOMAT. CoinFund led a $32M Series A in Trace Finance, building bank-grade stablecoin rails for Latin America. ([Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/top-new-stablecoins-to-watch-in-june-2026/), [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/reports/crypto-market-recap-q2-2026))

### Prediction markets reach infrastructure status
Prediction-market volumes in crypto wallets crossed $24 billion monthly (April figure) with a CFTC federal rulemaking underway. Wallet products integrating prediction markets are now building KYC, AML, geo-restrictions, and market-eligibility controls into their architecture from day one. ([Antier](https://www.antier.com/blogs/prediction-markets-in-crypto-wallets-the-web3-wallet-feature-nobody-saw-coming/))

### Markets and funding
Bitcoin rebounded ~4% to above $61,000 on dovish Fed signals. Venture funding remains slow overall, though social trading platform Fomo raised $75 million. NFT marketplaces posted an 82% fee drop in Q2; surviving NFT projects are those tied to real utility — gaming assets, tokenized RWAs with legal backing, and brand loyalty programs. ([Forbes Digital Assets](https://www.forbes.com/digital-assets/news/), [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/reports/crypto-market-recap-q2-2026), [EarnPark](https://earnpark.com/en/posts/nft-market-2026-dead-or-just-different/))
