# Web3 Weekly Update — June 12, 2026

## Highlights

- **Solana's Alpenglow goes live on testnet**: The largest core upgrade in Solana's history — replacing TowerBFT and Proof-of-History with Votor and Rotor — targets 150ms block finality, down from ~12 seconds.
- **CME and Nasdaq launch crypto index futures**: Cash-settled Nasdaq CME Crypto Index Futures began trading June 8 (announced June 10), tracking a market-cap-weighted basket of eight major cryptocurrencies.
- **"Web3 is dead" debate reignites**: Investor Kyle Samani declared on June 1 that only DeFi and DePIN remain, crystallizing a year in which growth has concentrated in ETFs, stablecoins, tokenized assets, and regulated finance.

## Detailed Updates

### Alpenglow: Solana's consensus overhaul reaches testnet
Solana's biggest-ever upgrade is live on a test cluster. Votor streamlines block voting into faster rounds; Rotor replaces the data broadcast layer. Together they aim for ~150 millisecond finality, near-instant transactions, and a cheaper, more stable network — a fundamental redesign rather than an incremental release. (Source: TheStreet Crypto)

### Institutional rails keep building
CME Group and Nasdaq launched cash-settled crypto index futures covering eight majors including BTC, ETH, and SOL (trading from June 8). Taurus partnered with P2P.org (June 10) to give banks direct access to Ethereum and Solana staking through its Swiss custody platform. The BIS published a June 1 report on stablecoins' global monetary impact, and Fireblocks launched Fireblocks Flow (June 2), stablecoin acceptance infrastructure integrating 800+ wallets that lets merchants accept any digital asset and settle in their chosen stablecoin. (Sources: CoinMarketCap, AlexaBlockchain, CryptoSlate)

### Samani: "Web3 is dead. All we have is DeFi and DePIN"
The Solana treasury chairman's June 1 post argued the Web3 umbrella has collapsed into two durable categories. Supporting his point, 2026's new capital and product growth has clustered around ETFs, tokenized assets, stablecoins, and regulated finance firms rather than consumer Web3. (Sources: crypto.news, U.Today)

### Regulation: UK FCA consultation closes; Asia-Pacific moves to production
The FCA's consultation on cryptoasset perimeter guidance closed June 3; if finalized as drafted, it could bring a large share of global Web3 interfaces and wallets within UK licensing scope, with final guidance expected in September. Meanwhile Japan's regulatory clarity is accelerating institutional DeFi in Asia-Pacific, Singapore's Project Guardian has expanded to production-scale tokenization, and South Korea's Virtual Asset User Protection Act is admitting compliant DeFi protocols. (Sources: Fintech & Digital Assets blog, Blocsys)

### Ethereum roadmap: Glamsterdam then Hegota
Ethereum's 2026 development centers on two upgrades — Glamsterdam (H1) and Hegota (H2) — reflecting a shift to smaller, more frequent releases over bundled high-risk hard forks. (Source: Tatum)

### NFT market: recovery, but thin
Early-2026 price and volume rebounds look less like a broad revival than rotation among existing funds amid extreme illiquidity. The surviving cohort is utility-focused: ticketing (GET Protocol) and loyalty ecosystems (Starbucks Odyssey) continue to scale actual users. (Sources: PANews, Earnpark)
