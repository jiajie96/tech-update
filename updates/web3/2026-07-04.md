# Web3 Weekly Update — July 4, 2026

## Highlights

- **GENIUS Act rules due July 18**: US federal and state regulators must finalize stablecoin regulations within two weeks, including licensing for federal issuers and criteria for foreign issuers — the most consequential US crypto-regulatory deadline of 2026.
- **Stablecoins go mainstream-rails**: Visa added five more blockchains to its stablecoin settlement pilot (now nine total), and Meta began offering stablecoin payouts to a limited group of creators.
- **Brokers race into DeFi**: eToro invested in onchain derivatives platform Extended and plans perpetual futures inside Zengo wallet, while Uniswap gained on a Robinhood link-up.

## Detailed Updates

### Countdown to GENIUS Act implementation
Final regulations implementing the GENIUS Act are due July 18, covering licensing of federal stablecoin issuers and market-access criteria for foreign issuers. The industry enters mid-July balancing deeper financial integration against tighter scrutiny — the week's Visa and Meta moves show integration isn't waiting for the rules. (Latham & Watkins tracker, PYMNTS)

### Visa and Meta expand stablecoin rails
Visa's global stablecoin settlement pilot now spans nine blockchains after adding five this week. Meta started stablecoin payouts for a limited creator group — a notable re-entry into crypto payments years after Diem's collapse. (PYMNTS)

### Brokers push onchain
eToro invested in onchain derivatives platform Extended, planning perpetual futures in the Zengo wallet and expanded DeFi products on its core platform, chasing Robinhood's onchain expansion. Uniswap rallied on its Robinhood integration as weak US jobs data buoyed crypto broadly. (The Block, CoinDesk)

### H2 upgrade season: Glamsterdam and Alpenglow
Ethereum's Glamsterdam — its most significant upgrade since The Merge — brings enshrined proposer-builder separation, expanded data throughput, and reduced database bloat, targeting H2 2026 mainnet. Solana's Alpenglow overhaul, shipping with Agave 4.1, replaces TowerBFT with the Votor voting component and aims to cut finality from ~12.8 seconds to 100-150 milliseconds. Base recently completed its Beryl hard fork. (Cointelegraph/TradingView, MEXC News)

### RWA tokenization overtakes DeFi in startup focus
The State of Web3 Capital 2026 report finds startups increasingly building for traditional financial markets: real-world asset tokenization is now the top focus at 29% of applications, ahead of DeFi at 23% — "Web3 founders are choosing Wall Street over DeFi." (CCN)

### Enforcement: Treasury sanctions ISIS-K addresses
The US Treasury sanctioned over 100 ISIS-K-linked crypto addresses that moved more than $1.4 million, a reminder that sanctions enforcement is scaling alongside legitimization. Separately, prediction markets — $63.5B notional volume in 2025, 4x 2024 — are becoming the highest-retention feature in Web3 wallets. (blog.mean.ceo, Antier)
