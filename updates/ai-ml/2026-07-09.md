# AI & Machine Learning Weekly Update — July 9, 2026

## Highlights

- **OpenAI's GPT-5.6 series nears broad release**: GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna have launched with broad access expected mid-July. Terra ($2.50/$15 per M tokens) directly challenges Anthropic's Sonnet 5 introductory pricing, while Luna ($1/$6) targets the volume tier. As of July 8 the models remain limited to ~20 government-vetted partner organizations.
- **Google delays Gemini 3.5 Pro to July 17 for full rebuild**: DeepMind scrapped the 2.5 Pro architecture entirely, rebuilding for improvements in mathematical reasoning, SVG scene generation, and image quality to compete with GPT-5.6 and Anthropic's Fable 5.
- **Anthropic overtakes OpenAI on revenue trajectory**: Anthropic is on course to hit $47B in revenue and reach profitability in 2029 — a year ahead of OpenAI — while ChatGPT's share of generative AI visits fell below a market majority for the first time.

## Detailed Updates

### OpenAI rolls out GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna
OpenAI launched its three-tier GPT-5.6 family, with broad access expected in mid-July 2026. Terra is positioned against Claude Sonnet 5's pricing, and Luna redefines the low-cost volume tier at $1/$6 per million tokens. Access is currently restricted to roughly 20 government-vetted partner organizations, making the staged rollout one of the most watched releases of the summer. GPT-5.6 also now powers OpenAI Codex. (llm-stats.com, buildfastwithai.com)

### Anthropic restores Fable 5 globally, launches Sonnet 5
Anthropic restored Fable 5 and Mythos 5 globally on July 1 after US export controls were lifted, adding cybersecurity classifiers described as its strongest safeguards. The company also launched Claude Sonnet 5 (which replaced Sonnet 4.6 on June 30) with near-Opus 4.8 performance at introductory $2/$10 per-million pricing through August 31; Fable 5 usage is priced at $10/$50 per million tokens as of July 8. (thursdai.news, llm-stats.com)

### Google DeepMind pushes Gemini 3.5 Pro to July 17
Google delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro's launch to July 17 after deciding to scrap the existing 2.5 Pro architecture for a complete rebuild. The model has already missed two self-imposed deadlines (the June I/O promise and a June 30 GA target) and remains in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview. Targets include stronger mathematical reasoning and image quality. (BigGo Finance)

### Grok 4.5 enters private beta
Elon Musk announced on June 28 that Grok 4.5, built on a 1.5T-parameter V9 foundation model, entered private beta at SpaceX and Tesla. Given xAI's monthly release cadence, a public launch in July is considered the most plausible window. (llm-stats.com)

### Chinese models capture 30–46% of enterprise API usage
Chinese AI models now account for between 30% and 46% of enterprise API token usage through US developer platforms, with share above 30% on OpenRouter since February. Z.ai's GLM-5.2 saw the fastest adoption of any model in 2026, helped by open-source Chinese models being 60–90% cheaper than leading Anthropic and OpenAI offerings. (llm-stats.com)

### Talent shake-up: Shazeer to OpenAI, Jumper to Anthropic
In consequential personnel moves, Noam Shazeer — Gemini co-lead and "Attention Is All You Need" co-author — announced he is joining OpenAI, while Nobel laureate John Jumper, lead architect of AlphaFold, departed DeepMind for Anthropic the following day. The moves underline how fierce the competition for senior research talent has become. (opendatascience.com)

### Generative AI adoption hits 53% of the population
New analysis shows generative AI reached 53% population adoption within three years — faster than the PC or the internet. The estimated value of generative AI tools to US consumers reached $172B annually by early 2026, with median per-user value tripling between 2025 and 2026. Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index highlights the shift from model-centric innovation to system-level deployment and governance. (hai.stanford.edu)
