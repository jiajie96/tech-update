# AI & Machine Learning Weekly Update — July 4, 2026

## Highlights

- **Anthropic launches Claude Sonnet 5**: Released June 30, Anthropic's most agentic model yet can autonomously use tools like browsers and terminals while delivering near-Opus 4.8 performance at significantly lower cost. The launch coincided with the U.S. government lifting national security restrictions on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models.
- **OpenAI announces Jalapeño inference chip**: OpenAI unveiled its first custom AI inference chip this week, joining the growing list of labs moving silicon in-house to control costs and capacity ahead of its anticipated IPO.
- **MGX raises $49B AI fund**: The Abu Dhabi firm closed one of the largest funds ever dedicated to AI deals on July 2, cementing sovereign capital's central role in the AI buildout.

## Detailed Updates

### Claude Sonnet 5 ships as restrictions on Fable 5 lift
Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, positioning it as its most agentic model to date with autonomous browser and terminal use at a much lower price point than Opus 4.8. In parallel, the U.S. government lifted the national security restrictions that had forced Anthropic to pull its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models, following an open letter from cybersecurity researchers calling the original move dangerous. (llm-stats.com, dentro.de)

### OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 Sol and reveals custom silicon
OpenAI has been previewing GPT-5.6 Sol, its next-generation model, since June 26, and this week announced the Jalapeño custom inference chip. The moves come as OpenAI staffs up ahead of an IPO, including Dean Ball joining July 6 to lead a new Strategic Futures team reporting to Chief Strategy Officer Jason Kwon. (TechCrunch, dentro.de)

### Record H1 for AI funding: $510B globally
Crunchbase reported global venture funding hit a record $510 billion in H1 2026, surpassing all of 2025. Nearly 88% of AI-related funding ($319B) went to U.S. companies, with OpenAI and Anthropic alone accounting for $217 billion — 43% of all startup funding in the half. Q2 also produced the strongest exit market since 2021. (Crunchbase News)

### MGX closes $49B AI fund; Together AI hits $8.3B valuation
Abu Dhabi's MGX raised $49 billion for AI deals, one of the biggest dedicated AI funds ever. Separately, neocloud Together AI raised an $800M Series C at an $8.3B valuation on July 1, underscoring continued appetite for GPU infrastructure plays. (Bloomberg, TechCrunch)

### Infrastructure race accelerates
Blackstone committed $30 billion to AI data centers in Japan, and Qualcomm acquired AI startup Modular for $4 billion, as the hardware and infrastructure buildout continues to outpace every other segment of the industry. (dentro.de)

### Google DeepMind talent drain continues
Six Google DeepMind researchers have departed for Meta, OpenAI, and Anthropic over five months, spanning reasoning, architecture, biology, coding, and pretraining — core areas of a frontier lab's stack. Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image on June 23 amid the churn. (X/Evan Kirstel, llm-stats.com)
