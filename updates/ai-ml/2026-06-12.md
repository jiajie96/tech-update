# AI & Machine Learning Weekly Update — June 12, 2026

## Highlights

- **Anthropic files for IPO at $965B valuation**: Anthropic closed a financing round valuing the company at $965 billion — surpassing OpenAI's private-market valuation — and confidentially filed for an IPO. Days earlier, the company published "When AI builds itself," a proposal for globally coordinated slowdown of frontier AI development.
- **OpenAI prepares its own IPO**: OpenAI is reportedly working with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley on a confidential filing, with a potential listing as soon as September 2026, following its record $122B raise at an $852B valuation.
- **Frontier lab CEOs ask Congress to mandate synthetic DNA screening**: The heads of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Microsoft AI signed a joint open letter to US Congress on June 5 calling for mandatory screening requirements on synthetic DNA providers — a rare coordinated biosecurity move.

## Detailed Updates

### Anthropic's dual move: IPO filing and a call for a global pause
Anthropic confirmed a financing round at a $965 billion valuation and a confidential IPO filing, while simultaneously publishing "When AI builds itself" (June 4), which proposes a globally coordinated pause or slowdown on frontier AI development as systems increasingly contribute to their own R&D. The juxtaposition of record valuation and governance caution dominated industry commentary this week. Separately, Andrej Karpathy announced he is joining Anthropic to return to frontier LLM R&D. (Sources: Shumaker client alert, dentro.de/ai, Amiko Consulting)

### OpenAI moves toward public markets
Following its $122 billion round (Amazon $50B, Nvidia $30B, SoftBank $30B), OpenAI is preparing to file confidentially for an IPO, with bankers at Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley and a possible September window. The company reports $2.6B monthly revenue and 900 million weekly ChatGPT users. It also launched Rosalind Biodefense on June 1, expanding trusted access to GPT-Rosalind for biodefense and pandemic-preparedness work. (Sources: dentro.de/ai, crescendo.ai)

### Joint letter on synthetic DNA screening
On June 5, the CEOs of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Microsoft AI jointly asked Congress to mandate screening for synthetic DNA orders — acknowledging growing dual-use risk as models become more capable in the life sciences. (Source: Yellow.com)

### New model releases
A dense fortnight of releases: Alibaba shipped Qwen3 Coder Next (June 6); MiniMax released its M2.5/M2.7 Highspeed line and M3; NVIDIA released Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B (June 4). Claude Opus 4.8 (May 28, $5/$25 per M tokens) now leads SWE-bench Verified at 88.6%, while Codex CLI on GPT-5.5 tops Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 83.4%. Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash, GA since I/O, received a refreshed build in early June to fix post-launch issues, and Google reset quota counters for all Gemini users. (Sources: llm-stats.com, pricepertoken.com, Developers Digest)

### Anthropic revenue run rate hits $30B
Dario Amodei revealed Claude reached a $30 billion revenue run rate by end of Q1 2026 — 80x annualized growth in a single quarter — underscoring how quickly enterprise AI spend is concentrating among frontier labs. (Source: crescendo.ai)

### Research: TurboQuant and the efficiency push
Google researchers presented TurboQuant, an algorithm that sharply reduces KV-cache memory overhead by combining PolarQuant vector rotation with Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss compression — part of a broader 2026 shift from model-centric innovation toward system-level efficiency, evaluation, and deployment. Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index similarly notes breakthrough scientific reasoning arriving alongside rising environmental cost. (Sources: JNGR 5.0 journal, Stanford HAI)
