# AI & Machine Learning Weekly Update — July 3, 2026

## Highlights

- **Anthropic launches Claude Sonnet 5**: Released June 30 and made the default model for Free and Pro users on July 1, Sonnet 5 is described as the most agentic Sonnet yet — it can autonomously use tools like browsers and terminals while delivering near-Opus 4.8 performance at lower cost than Sonnet 4.6 (introductory pricing runs through August 31).
- **Microsoft launches an AI deployment company**: Microsoft announced "Microsoft Frontier Company," a new operating business backed by $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers focused on getting enterprise AI deployments to actually succeed — days after AWS committed $1 billion to a similar forward-deployed-engineering venture.
- **OpenAI announces custom inference silicon**: OpenAI unveiled its "Jalapeño" inference chip on July 1, joining the wave of frontier labs moving inference workloads onto custom hardware to cut serving costs.

## Detailed Updates

### Claude Sonnet 5 arrives as the new default
Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, positioning it close to flagship Opus 4.8 performance on many tasks at a lower price than its predecessor. The model emphasizes autonomous tool use — browsers, terminals, and long-horizon agentic workflows — and became the default for Free and Pro users July 1. GitHub also added Sonnet 5 to Copilot for coding and agentic workflows. ([llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news), [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-1-2026))

### Google ships new image-generation models
Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash Image ($0.50/M input, $3.00/M output) and Gemini 3 Pro Image ($2.00/M input, $12.00/M output) on June 30, both available immediately via AI Studio and the Gemini API. DeepMind also launched Nano Banana 2 Lite, its fastest and cheapest image model, alongside Gemini Omni Flash. ([llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates))

### The "forward-deployed" enterprise AI race
Microsoft's new Frontier Company ($2.5B, 6,000 experts) and AWS's $1B internal deployment venture both embrace the forward-deployed engineer model pioneered by Palantir — a signal that hyperscalers now see hands-on deployment services, not just models and cloud capacity, as the bottleneck to enterprise AI value. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-launches-its-own-ai-deployment-company-with-2-5-billion-commitment/))

### Infrastructure spending accelerates
Blackstone committed $30 billion to AI data centers in Japan, and Qualcomm acquired AI startup Modular for $4 billion. South Korea announced an $880 billion, 10-year national plan spanning semiconductors, AI infrastructure, and robotics — the largest single national semiconductor investment announcement on record. ([dentro.de/ai](https://dentro.de/ai/news/), [unrot.co](https://unrot.co/blogs/today-top-10-ai-news-july-1-2026))

### MIT research roundup
Late-June MIT work included an algorithm-plus-hardware system that rapidly generates 3D navigation maps with minimal memory and power (June 25), a method that captures subtle atomic patterns to improve material-property predictions (June 23), and a spatial memory system that lets robots efficiently remember objects seen while exploring (June 17). ([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/topic/machine-learning))

### Attention-test study exposes LLM weakness
Researchers ran top models through a classic psychology attention test and found performance deteriorated sharply as color-naming lists grew longer and more complex — a reminder that frontier models still fail at sustained fine-grained attention even as reasoning benchmarks climb. ([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/artificial_intelligence/))

### Claude Fable 5 moves to API-only access
Anthropic's Mythos-class Fable 5 model left claude.ai Pro/Max plans on June 22 and is now a paid API tier at $10/M input and $50/M output tokens — a notable repositioning of top-tier capability as premium infrastructure. Meanwhile Zhipu AI's market cap crossed $128 billion on June 22 following its ZCode launch. ([Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/best-ai-coding-tools-june-2026-post-fable5), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/z-ai-launches-zcode-to-challenge-cursor-claude-code-and-github-copilot-in-ai-coding))
