# Gaming Weekly Update — July 5, 2026

## Highlights

- **Nintendo goes deep on Splatoon Raiders**: A dedicated Splatoon Raiders Direct aired June 30, detailing the single-player-focused Switch 2 spin-off ahead of its July 23 launch, plus a tie-in Splatfest and themed Joy-Con 2 controllers. It's Nintendo's biggest first-party push of the summer.
- **Palworld 1.0 locked for July 10**: Pocketpair confirmed final launch details this week for its exit from Early Access — Wing Pack aerial gear, the World Tree endgame zone, PvP, and roughly double the landmass, free for all existing owners. A rare Early Access mega-hit actually sticking the landing.
- **Rockstar under fire over labor practices**: A July 1 report accused Rockstar of ignoring pay inequity, mandating crunch, and weaponizing bonuses — reigniting the industry's long-running labor debate just as GTA VI anticipation peaks.

## Detailed Updates

### Splatoon Raiders Direct sets up Nintendo's summer tentpole
Nintendo held a dedicated Splatoon Raiders Direct on June 30, spotlighting the treasure-hunting spin-off launching exclusively on Switch 2 on July 23. Players take the role of a mechanic teaming up with the Deep Cut trio to explore the mysterious Spirhalite Islands, with optional co-op for up to four players. Nintendo also announced Deep Cut-themed Joy-Con 2 controllers launching alongside the game and a Splatoon 3 Splatfest running July 10–12. Coverage via [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/nintendo-direct/6-30-2026/) and [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/61674-splatoon-raiders-direct-announced-for-june-30th-2026-new-splatfest-also-announced).

### Palworld 1.0 details confirmed ahead of July 10 launch
Pocketpair confirmed this week that Palworld leaves Early Access on July 10 across Steam, PS5, Xbox, and Game Pass, free for existing owners. The 1.0 update — 27 pages of patch notes — adds the Wing Pack aerial traversal gear, the World Tree endgame zone, Sky Islands, a PvP mode, and Genetic Recombination breeding, roughly doubling the playable landmass versus the 2024 launch. Existing saves carry over, though a fresh start is recommended. Via [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319440/20260701/palworld-10-launches-july-10-wing-pack-world-tree-free-existing-players.htm) and [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/pc-game-release-dates-july-2026/).

### Rockstar accused of pay inequity and mandated crunch
Game Developer published a report on July 1 alleging Rockstar has ignored pay inequity complaints, mandated crunch, and used bonuses as leverage over staff. The allegations land at a sensitive moment for the GTA VI maker and add fuel to ongoing unionization and workplace-standards conversations across AAA development. Via [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/).

### July release slate: Doom and Black Flag remake headline a co-op-heavy month
The week's previews confirmed a front-loaded July: the new Doom entry arrives July 7, casting the Slayer into a "merciless purgatory," and Ubisoft's Assassin's Creed Black Flag remake follows on July 9. Outlets note the month is otherwise light on blockbusters but rich in co-op horror indies and major updates. Via [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-biggest-new-game-releases-of-july-2026/) and [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/pc-game-release-dates-july-2026/).

### Griffin Gaming Partners launches $100M indie fund
Per this month's indie development roundup, Griffin Gaming Partners launched a $100 million fund aimed specifically at indie studios, offering financing in exchange for revenue share rather than equity — a notable alternative funding model as traditional publisher deals remain scarce. Via [Indie Devs News](https://blog.mean.ceo/indie-devs-news-july-2026/).

### Switch 2's July lineup takes shape
A July 4 roundup positioned Splatoon Raiders as the anchor of five noteworthy Switch 2 releases this month, as Nintendo rides momentum from a fiscal year in which sales nearly doubled to $14.6 billion on the back of the Switch 2 launch. Via [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319719/20260704/nintendo-switch-2-july-2026-splatoon-raiders-leads-five-releases-worth-your-budget.htm).

### Engine watch: a quiet week, with big roadmaps still settling
No major engine releases shipped this week, but the industry is still digesting June's State of Unreal, where Epic laid out Unreal Engine 6 — unifying UE5 and Fortnite pipelines, shifting gameplay code toward Verse, with early access expected late 2026. Unity's AI suite (Assistant with Agent mode, MCP Server, asset Generators) continues its open beta rollout for Unity 6 users. Via [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/state-of-unreal-2026-top-news-from-the-show) and [Unity](https://unity.com/blog/unity-ai-how-to-get-started).
