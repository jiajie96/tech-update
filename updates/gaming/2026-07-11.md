# Gaming Weekly Update — July 11, 2026

## Highlights

- **Xbox confirms 3,200 layoffs in sweeping reset**: Xbox CEO Asha Sharma laid out the full reset plan, with 3,200 layoffs across FY'27 — 1,600 of which took effect Monday — marking one of the largest restructurings in the platform's history.
- **Sony to end physical disc production for new PlayStation games**: Sony Interactive Entertainment announced physical game disc production for all new PlayStation releases will be discontinued starting January 2028, a landmark moment in the industry's digital transition.
- **Palworld hits 1.0**: The creature-collecting survival crafter makes its full debut this month with new weapons and gear, a Wing Pack for aerial exploration, and additional story content.

## Detailed Updates

### Xbox's restructuring gets numbers
Asha Sharma detailed the full extent of the Xbox reset: 3,200 layoffs planned during FY'27, with 1,600 taking place Monday. The move reflects continued pressure on platform economics and a strategic pivot whose downstream effects on first-party studios remain to be seen. (Tabletop Battles industry roundup)

### Sony phases out physical discs for new releases
Sony announced that physical disc production for all new games releasing on PlayStation consoles will end starting January 2028. Combined with Nintendo discontinuing the original Switch 2 model in select regions (replacing it with a version featuring a replaceable battery), the console hardware landscape shifted notably this week. (Eneba weekly roundup)

### Slitherine picks up Blood Bowl
Slitherine acquired rights to publish Blood Bowl and will work with members of the Cyanide studio team on the license. More details are promised at Slitherine's Next Event showcase on July 22. Meanwhile Playground Productions continues reviving the Backyard Sports IP following its 2024 acquisition.

### July releases: Palworld 1.0 leads a quieter month
Palworld exits early access with its 1.0 release this month, adding new weapons, gear, the Wing Pack, and story-driven content. Also announced or dated: Hell Galaxy (dark sci-fi, 1.0 on Steam July 31) and Hillthorn, a wordless card-based investigation game from Raw Fury and developer Fool Moon. Commentators note July is light on blockbusters but strong for co-op horror. (GameSpot, PC Gamer)

### AI tooling continues to reshape development
Roughly 20% of new titles now openly disclose AI tool usage in their pipelines — asset generation, NPC dialogue, procedural narrative, or QA automation. Unity launched a new Unity AI beta with improved agentic capabilities and expanded generated asset types, and is preparing tech that generates "full casual games" from prompts. On the Unreal side, UE6's AI-assisted authoring (unveiled at June's State of Unreal, alongside plans to merge Fortnite's ecosystem) is expected to lower the barrier for portfolio-grade student work and reshape hiring pipelines. (Unity Discussions, PC Guide, tech-insider)
