# Gaming Weekly Update — June 12, 2026

## Highlights

- **Xbox Games Showcase: 25th anniversary hardware and the return of exclusives**: Xbox announced the Series X25 Limited Edition, confirmed Gears of War: E-Day (Oct 6) and Clockwork Revolution as true console exclusives, and dated Halo: Campaign Evolved for July 28.
- **Nintendo Direct teases Ocarina of Time remake**: Nintendo's first broadcast in months was headlined by an official tease of a remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, plus Rhythm Heaven Groove (July 2) and a reimagined Rayman (Oct 1).
- **Witcher 3 gets a new expansion — 11 years on**: CD Projekt Red announced Songs of the Past, co-developed with Fool's Theory, extending one of the most enduring RPGs ever shipped.

## Detailed Updates

### Xbox Games Showcase 2026
At its June 7 showcase, Xbox celebrated 25 years with the Series X25 Limited Edition console and X25 controller. Headline dates: Gears of War: E-Day launches October 6 with a world-premiere gameplay demo; Halo: Campaign Evolved hits PC and console July 28; Fable lands February 23, 2027; Resonance: A Plague Tale arrives August 27; Minecraft Dungeons 2 launches September 29. Gears E-Day and Clockwork Revolution were confirmed as full (not timed) exclusives, and a new hand-animated Cuphead game is in development. Persona 4 Revival comes February 18, 2027, and Resident Evil: Veronica targets 2027 across PS5, Xbox, Steam, and Switch 2. (Sources: Xbox Wire, GameSpot, Pure Xbox)

### Nintendo Direct
Nintendo's June Direct mixed third-party announcements with first-party surprises: the Ocarina of Time remake tease drew the most attention, alongside Rhythm Heaven Groove (80+ minigames, July 2) and Rayman's reimagined return (October 1). (Source: GameSpot)

### Witcher 3: Songs of the Past
CD Projekt Red announced a new Witcher 3 expansion co-developed with Fool's Theory — a notable bet on a 2015 title's continued commercial life while The Witcher 4 remains in development. (Source: Goonhammer roundup)

### Engines: Unity AI open beta; UE6 unifies stacks
Unity AI's open beta is now live for all developers on Unity 6+, enabling natural-language prompting of full casual game prototypes natively in-editor. Unreal Engine 6 details continue to emerge: unified Unreal/Fortnite tech stacks, AI-assisted authoring, and Nanite/Lumen extended to animated and dynamic geometry; MCP server integration is enabling teams to build agentic tooling directly into editor pipelines. Roughly 20% of new titles now disclose AI tool use somewhere in development. (Sources: Unity Discussions, practicetestgeeks, Summer Engine)

### Industry notes
Dark Horse Comics voluntarily recognized the Dark Horse Workers Union (formed May 27). This week's releases include Solarpunk (June 8), an eco-future survival-crafting title, and NBA The Run (June 9). (Sources: Goonhammer, DLCompare)
