# Gaming Weekly Update — July 9, 2026

## Highlights

- **Microsoft lays off 1,600 across Xbox**: The cuts include hundreds of unionized workers represented by the CWA, part of what new Xbox CEO Asha Sharma calls the division's "most significant restructure." Union leadership says it is "extremely disappointed" with Xbox leadership.
- **PlayStation to end physical disc production by 2028**: Sony announced it will phase out all physical game discs on its platform, triggering intense backlash — official PlayStation channels have gone silent across social platforms since the announcement.
- **Unity AI open beta promises prompt-to-game**: Unity's upgraded AI, now in open beta for Unity 6+, can generate full casual games from natural-language prompts, with Unity claiming its tech will soon eliminate the need for coding for simple titles.

## Detailed Updates

### Xbox restructure deepens with 1,600 layoffs
Microsoft laid off 1,600 employees across its Xbox division this week, including hundreds of union-represented video game workers. The CWA voiced strong criticism of Xbox leadership. The cuts come amid CEO Asha Sharma's sweeping restructure of the division, extending a multi-year pattern of contraction in AAA development. (Variety)

### PlayStation goes all-in on digital
PlayStation leadership announced plans to end all physical disc production for its games by 2028, citing the consumer shift to digital. The backlash has been severe enough that official PlayStation accounts have stopped posting entirely. The move raises preservation and ownership questions that will define platform debates for years. (tabletopbattles.com)

### Nintendo swaps Switch 2 models over battery regulation
Following updated regulations on replaceable batteries, Nintendo is discontinuing the original Switch 2 in selected regions, replacing it with a model featuring a user-replaceable battery — a notable example of hardware regulation directly reshaping console product lines. (tabletopbattles.com)

### Unity AI open beta: prompting casual games into existence
Unity's upgraded AI is now in open beta for all developers on Unity 6 and above. The system can generate full casual games from natural-language prompts alone, and Unity says its AI tooling will soon remove the need for coding for simple projects. Unity's next major version targets the same release window as Unreal Engine 6 with its own AI tooling and improved render pipelines. (gamedeveloper.com, discussions.unity.com)

### Unreal Engine 6: AI-assisted authoring, MCP integration
UE6's early-access preview is expected late 2026 (beta early 2027, stable ~late 2027), promising persistent internet-scale worlds and AI-assisted authoring designed to let students build portfolio-grade work in a semester. Unreal's Model Context Protocol integration is enabling teams to build agentic tooling directly into editor pipelines. Third-party tooling is moving fast too: Ramen's Aura 15.0 multi-agent assistant shipped with unlimited usage for both Unreal and Unity. (practicetestgeeks.com, businesswire.com)

### July releases: DOOM leads a co-op-heavy month
DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations launched July 7 with new missions, enemies, and oversized weaponry. July's release slate is otherwise light on blockbusters but heavy on co-op horror titles. (GameSpot, dlcompare.com)
