# Gaming Weekly Update — July 6, 2026

## Highlights

- **Xbox layoffs begin, studio closures on the table**: Microsoft's sweeping Xbox layoffs were set to start July 6, with reports that Arkane Studios may be shut down, the Blade game canceled, and Compulsion, Double Fine, Ninja Theory, and Undead Labs closed or sold.
- **Big release week**: Doom launches July 7, an enhanced Assassin's Creed Black Flag remake lands July 9, and Palworld 1.0 arrives July 10 across PC and consoles.
- **Unreal Engine 6 roadmap firms up**: Epic's unified engine — merging UE5 and UEFN — is expected in early-access preview late 2026, beta early 2027, and stable release around late 2027, with AI-assisted authoring as a headline feature.

## Detailed Updates

### Microsoft's July purge
As part of layoffs planned to start July 6, Microsoft is reportedly considering shutting down Arkane Studios and canceling Blade, while preparing to close or sell five studios: Compulsion Games, Double Fine, Ninja Theory, Undead Labs, and Arkane. Leadership outlined the overhaul in a June 10 letter to staff, with cuts timed after the fiscal year ended June 30. Analysts tie the pressure to the $68.7B Activision Blizzard acquisition. The pain extends beyond Microsoft: Bungie faces its third layoff round in three years (as early as July 9, ~300 Bellevue employees), and reports warn of July closures at Sony, EA, and BioWare. (Engadget, GameRant, TechTimes)

### Release week: Doom, Black Flag remake, Palworld 1.0
July 7 brings the new Doom to PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S; July 9 sees Ubisoft's enhanced remake of Assassin's Creed Black Flag; and Palworld exits early access with its 1.0 release July 10. PC Gamer notes July is otherwise light on blockbusters but strong for co-op horror. (GameSpot, PC Gamer)

### Unreal Engine 6: one engine, AI-assisted
Epic's Unreal Engine 6 will merge UE5 and UEFN into a single unified engine, with an early-access preview expected late 2026, beta in early 2027, and stable 6.0 around late 2027. AI-assisted authoring is positioned as a democratizing feature. Epic also reports over $1B paid out to Fortnite ecosystem developers. (gamedeveloper.com, tech-insider.org)

### Unity AI open beta expands
Unity AI's open beta is now live for Unity 6, giving developers an in-editor AI assistant for project workflows — part of the engine makers' race to embed generative tooling directly into pipelines. Third-party tools like Aura 15.0 (released June 26) now offer unlimited-usage AI features across both Unreal and Unity. (Unity Discussions, BusinessWire)

### AI in games: adoption up, sentiment down
In 2026, 62% of new RPG/adventure games feature AI-powered NPCs, up from 8% in 2024. NVIDIA ACE now runs on-device and shipped a production-grade TTS model at GDC 2026, and the AI game-assets market is valued at $2.08B (projected $10.73B by 2035). Yet developer sentiment is souring: 52% of developers now view generative AI negatively, up from 30% a year earlier. (blog.imseankim.com, thegww.com)
